## 5. Path after-sale methodology

SOURCE IMPLEMENTED. Target is the sell date plus 1, 3, 6, or 12 calendar months. The print is the first session on or after that date, within 10 days, never before the target, and never today’s price if the window has not arrived. A raw/adjusted ratio change above 2% uses the adjusted series or `CORPORATE_ACTION_UNCHECKED`. Missing cells carry a stable code: `INVALID_SELL`, `INVALID_SELL_DATE`, `NO_HISTORICAL_DATA`, `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`, `NO_SESSION_IN_WINDOW`, `CORPORATE_ACTION_UNCHECKED`, `CALCULATED`, `AI_SOURCE`.

The main cell is a percentage or an em dash. Target date, observed date, observed price, basis, code, and source are on Details (and the em dash tooltip).

ACTUALLY VERIFIED. Path page in the browser shows the after-sale table with Details, not calculation text inside the percentage cells. Sample ITC row is present. Codes are covered by `path.test.ts`.

## 6. Path AI fallback

SOURCE IMPLEMENTED. Order stays deterministic first. “Complete Path data” may ask for a close only when the cell is `NO_HISTORICAL_DATA`, at most 6 cells, and only stores a price when `usablePathPrice` accepts it: researched, http(s) source, source name, evidence of at least 8 characters that includes the exact date, finite positive price, and no estimate / interpolation / prediction / guess. Identity lookup still calls `usablePathPrice` without a date and gets null. Stored as `AI-researched · source-backed` (`status: "ai"`, `code: AI_SOURCE`, `basis: "source"`). Returns are `(sourced close / sell price − 1)`.

ACTUALLY VERIFIED by `complete.test.ts` and `path.test.ts`. A live model call was not made in this session. The button and the “nothing is estimated” copy are on the Path page.

## 22. Remaining limitations

- Yahoo is delayed. LIVE appears only if a quote reports `delayMin === 0`. That did not happen on 4 Oct 2026.
- Sunday session: status is LAST AVAILABLE, not a live open.
- Ambiguous numeric dates (both parts ≤ 12) stay Indian DD/MM so Zerodha-style files are not dropped. They are not flagged as unknown.
- Invalid quantities are skipped and counted as ignored. They are not stored as zero. There is no separate “bad quantity” column.
- AI historical closes are capped at 6 per click and only fill `NO_HISTORICAL_DATA`. Gaps inside an existing series are not sent to a model.
- Corporate actions are detected from the raw vs adjusted series. None are invented from a name change.
