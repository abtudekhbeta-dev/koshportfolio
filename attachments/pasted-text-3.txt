KOSH — LIQUIDITY & REVERSAL SCREENER: TECHNICAL AUDIT AND CORRECTION

Review and improve the existing implementation in the GitHub repository. Do not rebuild the feature or replace its architecture. Preserve the existing UI, shared detection engine, chart integration, server-side scanning and persistence wherever they are working correctly.

The review must address the following issues.

1. Complete stock universe

The current All NSE universe is not equivalent to the requested full NSE+BSE universe.

- Include all supported NSE and BSE equity listings for which reliable OHLC data is available.
- Include BSE-only listings, not just ".BO" symbols manually added to watchlists.
- Preserve exchange-specific identity and prevent accidental duplication of the same listing.
- Distinguish unavailable data from a stock that genuinely has no signal.
- Show actual scanned, skipped and data-available counts.
- Never claim full-universe coverage when only a subset was scanned.

2. Correct signal lifecycle

Explicitly distinguish:

- Level identified.
- Sweep developing.
- Sweep confirmed.
- Reclaim developing.
- Reclaim confirmed.
- Directional confirmation.
- Invalidated.
- Expired.

A completed candle closing back through the original level is a confirmed reclaim. A later break of the reclaim candle's high/low is an additional directional confirmation, not a prerequisite for recognising the reclaim itself.

Do not incorrectly leave a valid reclaim classified as developing merely because the subsequent directional confirmation has not occurred.

Ensure intrabar signals remain provisional and cannot be presented as completed-candle signals.

3. Improve Strong and Potential classification

Keep structural validity separate from supporting evidence.

A genuine penetration and reclaim must remain essential to the precise liquidity sweep screener. A normal support bounce must never qualify.

Missing volume must be treated as unavailable evidence, not as positive volume confirmation.

Review the current score calculation against the intended weights:

- Swing significance: 20
- Sweep quality: 20
- Reclaim strength: 20
- Relative volume: 15
- Candle structure: 15
- Subsequent directional confirmation: 10

Retain the score as a ranking measure, not a probability.

A shallow penetration below the minimum threshold should not receive a Strong label simply because other score components are high.

4. Rework historical performance statistics

Calculate statistics from individual signal observations across the entire scan, not from medians or summary statistics of batches.

For every pattern and direction, report:

- Number of valid historical signals.
- Number with a completed 1, 3, 5 and 8-candle forward window.
- Average and median forward return at each horizon.
- Median favourable and adverse excursion.
- Invalidation rate.
- Results separately for Strong and Potential signals, wherever sample size allows.

Use the actual reclaim close as the measurement base. Define bullish and bearish MFE/MAE consistently.

Do not mix incomplete forward windows into completed-horizon statistics. Clearly identify overlapping signals and avoid treating them as independent trades.

5. Audit all timeframes

Review 15m, 1H, 4H, Daily, Weekly and Monthly construction.

In particular:

- Verify the IST session boundaries.
- Verify the custom 4H aggregation and clearly identify the shorter afternoon session bar.
- Confirm that a closed candle is not mistakenly treated as forming.
- Confirm that weekends and market holidays do not produce false new bars.
- Verify that the current weekly and monthly candles are handled correctly.
- Identify insufficient historical depth rather than silently lowering requirements.

6. Historical replay and no-look-ahead verification

Implement or strengthen sequential historical replay tests.

At every historical point, the detector must have access only to candles that would actually have been available at that time.

Test whether:

- A swing is only available after its right-side candles close.
- An intrabar signal can disappear before candle close.
- A reclaim is not labelled before the candle closes.
- Invalidation is applied only after the relevant subsequent candle.
- Historical statistics do not accidentally use future information to select signals.

7. Scan reliability and persistence

Test:

- Full scan completion across large universes.
- Resume after interruption.
- Concurrent scan requests.
- Duplicate prevention across batches.
- Cache freshness and invalidation.
- Watchlist changes.
- SQL persistence and memory fallback.

Make sure a partial scan is never presented as a completed full-universe scan.

8. UI and user-facing output

Preserve the current interface unless a change is needed to communicate accuracy.

Show:

- Universe and actual coverage.
- Timeframe and data freshness.
- Strong and Potential results separately.
- Developing and Confirmed states separately.
- Reclaim and directional confirmation separately.
- Actual skipped and scanned counts.

Do not show technical implementation errors or source-provider internals to ordinary users.

9. Final acceptance report

After completing the corrections, report:

- Files changed.
- Actual NSE and BSE coverage.
- Historical data depth by timeframe.
- Test cases added and results.
- Historical replay results.
- Forward statistics validation.
- Remaining limitations.

Do not report a feature or test as completed unless it has actually been implemented and verified.

Preserve all existing working Kosh functionality. Run the relevant automated tests, typecheck and production build.
