Analysis modes

One canonical dataset; two distinct reasoning layers.

Kosh rules + Grok AI

Deterministic verdict. Describe flow direction, magnitude, persistence, divergence and index trend using transparent formulas. Explain the evidence and counter-evidence.

What it can establish: foreign net selling coincided with domestic net buying and the index declined over this period.

What it cannot establish: which group caused the move, whether retail investors panicked, or when a recovery will happen.

Rules-based checks

Multi-timeframe synthesis. Grok interprets daily, weekly and monthly flows alongside broader market performance, breadth, sector leadership, volatility and reliable global/macro context.

Historical analogue evidence. Compare multiple matched periods—including cases that failed to recover—and show exact dates, match factors, sample size and actual forward returns/drawdowns computed by code.

Conditional outlook. Explain scenarios, supporting and contradicting evidence, triggers and invalidation conditions. Do not turn historical similarity into certainty.

Scenario framework · no fake probabilities

Stabilisation / recoveryWould gain support if domestic absorption persists, foreign selling moderates, and breadth and price trend improve.
Continued weaknessWould gain support if selling persists while breadth, volatility and price trend deteriorate.
Range / whipsawWould remain plausible if flows diverge and price/breadth confirmation is inconsistent.
Historical analogues are evidence, not forecasts. Probabilities may only be shown after calibration and out-of-sample validation. Do not use AI self-rated confidence as a probability.
Implementation principle: official, accessible sources first; data quality before breadth; code calculates facts and forward outcomes; AI interprets those facts. All values in this reference are fictional placeholders and must never be shipped as production data.
